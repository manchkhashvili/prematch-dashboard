Books

Turning a book off stops its next poll entirely — no fetch, no parse, no tick write, no ladder-anomaly pass — and clears its odds so nothing downstream prices off a frozen snapshot. Takes effect within 5s. Pinnacle is the reference and is always on.

Scans

The anomaly scans are the expensive ones: a cold CrystalBet full-detail soccer sweep is ~278s of CPU. Turn them off when you're not watching.

Cadence (seconds)

How often each loop runs. Longer = cheaper. Clamped server-side.

Work limits

Horizons that cut per-cycle work without turning a book off. Only ~39% of the soccer board starts within 24h, so a shorter horizon drops a large share of the expansion/decode cost.
