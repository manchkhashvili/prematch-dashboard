Half-time & HT/FT from the full-time market

Fits Poisson goal rates through a two-half model so they always reproduce the full-time prices you enter, then reads off the half-time and HT/FT markets. Because FT 1X2 plus one total is 3 constraints on 2 rates, it sweeps the assumption space (devig × fit × H1 goal share × H2 score-dependence) and reports a band, not a point estimate. The half-time price is not an input — that is the point: it is usually the market the book does not post, or posts lazily.
